Privacy Policy

Waia: AI Wealth Assistant • Effective: November 1, 2026

Waia is a personal finance tracking app with an optional AI Coach and social features. This policy explains what data we process, who receives it and the choices you have. The data controller is Gökhan Azap. Contact: support@getwaia.com.

In short: we do not sell your personal data. Waia uses service providers for hosting, AI, purchases, product analytics, error reporting and advertising measurement. You can delete your account in the app.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account and profile: email and sign-in information, user ID, display name, username, photo, bio, city, occupation and preferences.
  • Financial records: assets, liabilities, portfolios, net worth history, goals, income and expenses, notes, recurring items, budgets and watchlists. These come from your entries, AI Coach actions or connections you choose.
  • AI Coach: messages, conversation history, voice messages, photos and PDF statements you send, and the financial context needed to answer you.
  • Social and support: posts, comments, likes, follows, images, reports, blocks, feedback and messages to support.
  • Purchases: store and Adapty identifiers, products, transaction, trial, renewal, cancellation and refund status, price and store country. Apple or Google handles payment; we do not receive your full card details.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, device, operating system and app version, language, timezone, app and SDK identifiers, screens and features used, purchase steps, error reports and push tokens. Advertising identifiers are collected only where your device settings allow it.

2. How We Use Data

We use data to run your account, save and calculate your records, answer AI Coach requests, show market data and social content, sync connections you choose, send notifications, manage purchases, provide support, fix errors, prevent abuse, improve the app and measure our advertising.

3. What Other People Can See

Your profile, posts, comments, likes and follows are visible to others. Your public profile record can also include account details such as plan or trial status and timezone. Do not post account numbers, passwords or statements.

Portfolio sharing is off by default. If you turn it on, others can see your latest net worth and your allocation percentages. Turning it off stops this access but cannot recall copies others already made.

Your transactions, budgets, detailed holdings and liabilities are private to your account. Profile, post and goal images use public links that anyone with the link can open.

4. AI Coach

Before your first AI Coach use, Waia asks for your consent to send data to Google Gemini. You can revoke it in Settings at any time; this stops future AI requests but does not erase earlier conversations or copies a provider already processed.

With your consent, Gemini receives your messages, relevant conversation history, voice messages, photos and PDFs you send, and relevant financial details such as assets, balances, liabilities, goals and transactions. Statements and photos can contain account details and information about other people, so remove what is not needed. The AI Coach can add, change or delete records when you ask it to, so review its work.

5. Who Receives Data

  • Supabase: login, database, file storage and backend functions. Our database is hosted in Sydney, Australia.
  • Google Gemini: AI Coach requests, as described above.
  • Apple, Google Play and Adapty: purchases, subscriptions, offers and restoring access.
  • PostHog: product analytics, including screens, feature use and purchase steps. It can be linked to your user ID after you sign in.
  • Sentry: crash and error reports, including device information. Reports can be linked to your user ID and email after you sign in.
  • Meta: app activity, registration, checkout, trial and purchase events with app or SDK identifiers, and advertising identifiers where allowed, to measure our advertising. Some of these events reach Meta through Adapty.
  • Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko, Logo.dev, Google favicon services, flagcdn.com and jsDelivr: prices, symbols, logos, flags and images. These requests reveal what is requested and your network information, not your financial records.
  • Connections you choose: Plaid, supported exchanges and wallet lookup services such as Moralis and Blockstream process the data needed for that connection. We store the connection details and the records they sync.
  • Expo, Apple and Google: delivery of push notifications.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

6. Tracking and Device Choices

On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt controls access to your advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline, we do not access the IDFA, but app and purchase events with app or SDK identifiers can still be sent to the providers above, including Meta through Adapty, and analytics and error reporting continue. On Android, your device settings control the advertising ID.

You can refuse microphone, camera, photo and notification access and still use the features that do not need them. Waia does not ask for GPS or contacts access.

7. International Transfers

Your data is processed outside your country, including in Australia (Supabase) and the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Privacy protections can differ between countries. Contact us for details about the transfer arrangements for a specific provider.

8. Security and Retention

We use encrypted connections and access rules on our database. No system is completely secure, and Waia does not use end-to-end encryption. Some data is also stored on your device.

We keep your data while your account is active unless you delete it. Provider copies, backups, and purchase and legal records can be kept longer under their own rules.

9. Deleting Your Data

You can edit or delete your records and conversations in the app, turn off portfolio sharing, revoke AI consent and disconnect connections. Disconnecting does not erase records already imported.

You can delete your account in Settings. This removes your login and related database records and attempts to remove your uploaded files. Contact support@getwaia.com if any files remain, to ask about analytics or provider records, or if you bought without an account. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in your Apple or Google settings.

10. Your Rights

Contact support@getwaia.com to exercise your rights. We may ask for information to verify your request. Never send passwords or full card numbers.

Under Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), you can learn whether and why your data is processed and who receives it in Türkiye or abroad, ask for correction or deletion where the legal conditions are met, object to an adverse result based only on automated analysis, and seek compensation for damage from unlawful processing. Other laws may give you further rights, such as access, a portable copy or objection. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or another competent authority.

11. Minimum Age

Waia is for users aged 17 and over. If you believe someone younger has given us personal data, contact support@getwaia.com.

12. Changes

When we update this policy we change the date shown here and give appropriate notice of material changes.
