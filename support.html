Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add an asset?

Go to the Wealth tab and tap "Add Asset." You can add stocks, crypto, real estate, cash, and more.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored securely with end-to-end encryption. We never sell or share your personal financial information.

How does the AI Coach work?

The AI Coach uses Google Gemini to help you manage your portfolio through natural language. You can add transactions, ask questions about your finances, and get insights — all by chatting.

Can I track expenses and income?

Yes. The Cashflow tab lets you log income and expenses, track subscriptions, set budgets, and visualize your money flow.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Wealth tab, tap Settings (gear icon), scroll down, and tap "Delete Account." This will permanently remove all your data.

Is market data real-time?

Market data may be delayed by 15-20 minutes. Waia is designed for portfolio tracking, not real-time trading.
