Delete Your Waia Account

Permanently remove your Waia account and all associated data.

App Information

App: Waia — AI Wealth Assistant
Developer: Gokhan Azap

Option 1 — Delete from Inside the App (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the Waia app:

  1. Open the Waia app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Wealth tab (last tab on the right).
  3. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon, top right).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm deletion. Your account and all associated data will be permanently removed.

Option 2 — Request Deletion via Email

If you can no longer access the app, you can request account deletion by email:

Send an email to feedback@waia.app from the email address associated with your Waia account.

Subject: Account Deletion Request

We will verify your identity and process your request within 14 days.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your profile information (name, username, email, profile photo)
  • All financial data (assets, liabilities, goals, transactions, net worth snapshots, portfolios)
  • All social activity (posts, comments, likes, follows, watchlist items)
  • AI Coach chat history
  • Connected bank accounts, crypto wallets, and exchange links
  • Push notification tokens
  • Subscription records (note: cancel your active subscription before deleting your account; see below)

What Is Retained

For legal, security, and fraud-prevention reasons, a limited amount of data may be retained:

  • Anonymized analytics — aggregated, non-identifying usage data with no link back to you.
  • Transaction records — for any purchases or subscriptions, billing records are retained as required by tax and accounting law (typically 7–10 years), but contain only the transaction itself, not your app data.
  • Abuse and security logs — if your account was reported or blocked for policy violations, a minimal record may be retained to prevent re-registration. Retention: up to 12 months.

All retained data is decoupled from your personal identity wherever legally permitted.

Important: If you have an active Waia Pro subscription, please cancel it through your Apple ID subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions before deleting your account. Deleting your Waia account does not automatically cancel your subscription with Apple or Google.
